Good questions

Everything you're wondering

01 What is Smooch?

Smooch is a daily gummy formulated to support desire, mood, and connection — a small everyday ritual, not a quick fix. As with any supplement, experiences vary.

02 Who is it for?

Women who want to make a little daily space for desire, mood, and confidence. It's a wellness supplement, not a treatment — if you have a specific health concern, a qualified healthcare professional is the right first stop.

03 How do I take it?

Take the recommended serving once a day — you'll find the exact serving size on the label. With or without food, whichever suits you.

04 When should I take it?

Whenever you'll actually remember it. Most people anchor it to something they already do — morning coffee, the evening wind-down. Consistency matters more than the clock.

05 How long should I use it?

Smooch is designed for consistent, ongoing use. Give it several consistent weeks before you judge it, then check in with how you feel. Experiences vary — some people notice subtle shifts sooner than others.

06 What's inside?

Every ingredient is listed in the formula section on our product page, and the final ingredient list always appears on the label — no hidden blends.

07 Can I combine it with other supplements?

That depends on what else you're taking. Before combining Smooch with other supplements, talk with a qualified healthcare professional who can look at your full routine.

08 Can I use it while pregnant or breastfeeding?

Do not use Smooch while pregnant or breastfeeding without talking to a qualified healthcare professional first.

09 Can I take it with medication?

If you take any medication, talk with a qualified healthcare professional before starting Smooch. They know your full picture — we don't.

10 How does the subscription work?

Choose a delivery schedule at checkout and your order arrives automatically, with any subscription savings applied to each delivery. You stay in control and can manage or cancel at any time.

11 How do I cancel?

Anytime, in a couple of clicks — use the manage link in your order emails, or message our support team and we'll take care of it. No hoops, no guilt.

12 What's the return policy?

Returns and refunds are handled under our refund policy — you’ll find the full details linked in the footer, and our support team is happy to help.
