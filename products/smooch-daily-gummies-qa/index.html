The Science Behind Our Formula

Stress & Mood

What's Happening

Desire runs on bandwidth. When your brain reads the day as one long to-do list, your nervous system stays on duty — and everything quieter gets muted. It isn't a lack of love, and nothing is broken: a mind that never clocks out simply has no room left for wanting anything.

How We Help

KSM-66 Ashwagandha — an adaptogenic root with a long history in Ayurvedic practice — is included to support stress resilience and a calmer everyday baseline. With daily consistency, Smooch supports the settled, present feeling that gives the rest of you room to show up.*

Desire & Energy

What's Happening

For many women, desire is responsive rather than spontaneous — it rarely shows up first. It tends to arrive after closeness begins, when there's no pressure attached. That's a normal desire style, not a flaw. But it needs somewhere to build: energy left at the end of the day, and a mind at ease enough to notice what it wants.

How We Help

Black Maca, a Peruvian root traditionally used to support desire and energy, is paired with Damiana, a botanical leaf with centuries of traditional use around relaxation and openness. Together they are Smooch's daily support for the part of you that wants to want.*

Connection & Presence

What's Happening

Presence is physical. When your mind is elsewhere — replaying the day, watching yourself, waiting for your body to catch up — closeness can feel mechanical instead of natural. Feeling connected to someone else starts with feeling at ease in your own body first.

How We Help

Smooch is built as one small daily ritual, not an in-the-moment fix: the full four-ingredient blend, finished with warming Cinnamon Bark, taken consistently over weeks. Quiet, steady support for feeling more like yourself — and more present when it matters.*

What's inside

Four ingredients. One easy ritual.

Every ingredient earns its place. No proprietary-blend hide-and-seek.

  • 01Desire

    Black Maca

    The want-to-want root: traditionally used to support desire and energy. Experiences vary.

    Why it’s in the formula

    A cruciferous root cultivated high in the Peruvian Andes for over two thousand years and traditionally taken daily for desire, energy, and stamina. Black maca is the rarest of the maca colors — the one tradition prized most — and Smooch uses gelatinized root, gently pre-cooked the traditional way so it suits an every-day ritual.

    KSM-66 Ashwagandha

    For the brain that never clocks out: traditionally used to support a calmer everyday baseline. Experiences vary.

    Why it’s in the formula

    Ashwagandha root has anchored Ayurvedic practice for over three thousand years as a daily tonic for stressful seasons. KSM-66 is a root-only, full-spectrum extract and one of the most researched forms of ashwagandha, studied in human trials in areas like stress resilience, calm, and everyday mood. It's the formula's steady base.

    Why it’s in the formula

    A small flowering shrub from Mexico and the American Southwest, damiana leaf has been brewed for centuries as a relaxing cordial — long associated in traditional practice with ease, openness, and romance. It carries the softer side of the formula: the unwind that makes room for wanting.
