02 Who is it for?

Women in a season where mood, stress, or desire feel quieter than usual — and who want daily support, not a diagnosis. It's yours alone: private, pressure-free, no partner required.

03 How long until I notice something?

Gradually, if at all — we won't invent a timeline. Change like this tends to be quiet and cumulative, often noticed only when you look back. The 30-day window exists so you can judge honestly.

Autoship renews at the price you chose, ships free, and takes a couple of clicks to pause, edit, or cancel — no phone call, no chat maze. Prefer a one-off? A single bottle is $39.95.

It is built to be the opposite. One private gummy a day, tied to a moment you already keep — support for you, never a demand on you. No one is watching, and nothing is owed.

06 Can I take it with medication, or while pregnant or nursing?

That's a clinician's call, and we mean that. All four ingredients are named on the label — bring it to your next appointment and ask.
